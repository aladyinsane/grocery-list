# ADR-0000: Title in the form of a decision

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forces a decision here? What are the constraints — technical, practical,
human? What did we know at the time? Cite the [principles](../PRINCIPLES.md) that apply.

State the problem, not the solution. If this section reads as an argument for the option
we picked, it's written wrong.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the active voice. "We will…"

Be specific enough that someone could implement it from this section alone.

## Consequences

What becomes true once this is in place — the good, the bad, and the merely different.

Include what this makes *harder*. An ADR with no downsides listed is an ADR that hasn't
been thought about.

## Alternatives considered

### Option we didn't pick

What it was, and the specific reason it lost. Not "it was worse" — what about it was worse,
and for whose benefit did we trade it away.
